About BillionBlogs

Financial journalism built to be understood, not skimmed.

We started BillionBlogs because most financial content is written to sell something. Ours is written to explain something.

BillionBlogs is an independent publication covering personal finance, investing, income, and the economics that shape everyday decisions. We report on the mechanics of money — how budgets actually behave, why smart people still make bad financial calls, what a rate change means for a mortgage — without the affiliate-link incentives that distort so much of the category.

Every piece runs through the same filter: would this help a reader make one better decision this month? If not, it doesn't publish. That standard means fewer articles and slower output than most finance sites — a trade we make deliberately.

How we work

Our editorial team writes from primary sources — Federal Reserve data, SEC filings, academic research — and discloses assumptions rather than hiding them behind confident language. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we mark sponsored content clearly when it appears.

What we're not

We're not a robo-advisor, a brokerage, or a substitute for a licensed financial planner. Nothing on BillionBlogs is personalized financial advice — it's context, so you can ask your advisor, or yourself, better questions.
